# Rabbit Health Tracker

Tracks the health of one or more pet rabbits so their owner can spot problems early — especially the
sudden changes in eating and droppings that signal a rabbit emergency.

## Language

**Observation**:
Something the owner noticed about a rabbit at a specific moment — droppings, appetite, mood, a
symptom. Recorded when noticed, not on a schedule; several may exist for one day, or none.
_Avoid_: health log, daily log, diary entry, check-in

**Archive**:
To hide a rabbit from everyday use while keeping all of its records. The opposite of deleting, which
destroys them. An archived rabbit has usually died or been rehomed.
_Avoid_: delete, remove, hide, deactivate

**Document**:
A scan or photo of paperwork from a vet — results, prescriptions, vaccination records — attached to a
rabbit and optionally to a visit. A record, not a memory: it is evidence the owner may need again.
_Avoid_: file, attachment, scan, paper

**Photo**:
A picture of a rabbit kept for its own sake, including the rabbit's avatar. Sentimental rather than
evidential, and the bulk of the app's storage.
_Avoid_: image, picture, media

**Medication Course**:
A medicine a rabbit is meant to take between a start and end date, optionally on a daily schedule of
clock times. Scheduling can be switched off, leaving a course the owner simply records doses against.
_Avoid_: prescription, treatment, med

**Dose**:
One administration of a medication course. A *due dose* is derived from the course schedule and does
not exist until acted on; a *recorded dose* is a stored fact that the owner gave it or deliberately
skipped it. A dose can be recorded without any schedule.
_Avoid_: intake, administration, pill

**Care Reminder**:
A prompt for recurring husbandry due around a date — nail trim, vaccination, routine weigh-in. Distinct
from a dose reminder, which belongs to a medication course and is time-critical.
_Avoid_: task, todo, alert, notification

**Watch**:
A period during which the owner has declared a rabbit needs closer attention. Only while a watch is
active does the app chase the owner for fresh observations. Off by default.
_Avoid_: alert mode, monitoring, sick mode, observation period

**Droppings**:
The ordinary hard round pellets a rabbit produces. How many, and how big, are the earliest signals that
something is wrong. An observation always carries an amount, defaulting to normal so a healthy day is one
tap; size, form and cecotropes are optional and mean unknown when untouched.
_Avoid_: poop, faeces, stool, pellets (ambiguous with food pellets)

**Symptom**:
A named sign of illness the owner can tick on an observation, drawn from a list that ships with the app
and that the owner can extend. Distinct from free-text notes, because a symptom can be counted over time.
_Avoid_: condition, issue, ailment, problem

**Together**:
Rabbits that share a living space and litter tray, as bonded rabbits normally do. Droppings from a shared
tray cannot be attributed to one rabbit, so an observation may cover several rabbits at once and is shown
as such.
_Avoid_: bonded pair, group, herd, cage mates

**Cecotrope**:
A soft nutrient-rich dropping a rabbit normally eats directly. Seeing them left uneaten is a signal
worth recording, and they are not the same thing as ordinary droppings.
_Avoid_: caecotroph, night faeces, soft poop
