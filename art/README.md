# Identity assets

Source art for the launcher icon and the Play listing. Everything in `app/src/main/res` that
draws a rabbit is **generated from here** — edit the source and regenerate, don't hand-edit the
path data in the `VectorDrawable`s.

| Asset | Source | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rabbit.py`'s MARK and EYE curves | `concept-1.png` → `trace-mark.py` | final |
| `drawable/ic_launcher_{foreground,monochrome}.xml`, `drawable/ic_notification.xml`, `mipmap-*`, `play-icon-512.png` | `rabbit.py` → `make-launcher-icon.py` | final |
| `play-feature-graphic.png` (1024×500) | `rabbit.py` → `make-feature-graphic.py` | final |
| `play-screenshot-{1-home,2-weight}.png` (1526×2713) | device capture → `pad-screenshot.py` | placeholder until 3g |

**No third-party art is involved, and nothing here carries a licence obligation.** That is a
deliberate property, not a coincidence — see below.

## The mark

`rabbit.py` is the single definition: a sitting rabbit facing right, as **two cubic subpaths** —
the silhouette and the eye. Both generators import it, so the launcher icon, the notification icon
and the feature graphic cannot drift into being different rabbits.

- The **rose** carries all the colour, because the rabbit is near-white. Recolouring the identity
  is **two** values that nothing checks against each other: `ROSE`/`ROSE_DARK` in `rabbit.py`
  (which drive the mipmaps, the Play icon and the feature graphic's gradient) *and* the matching
  `fillColor` in `drawable/ic_launcher_background.xml`, which `make-launcher-icon.py` deliberately
  never writes. Changing one alone is the failure to expect. This README used to claim the
  recolour was a single value; that stopped being true when the feature graphic gained a second
  gradient stop.
- The colours come from the Phase 7 light scheme rather than being picked for the icon:
  `#693238` is `primary` darkened until it holds 8.95:1 against the cream at icon sizes, and the
  cream is `surface`. The green it replaced (`#3D7A4F`) appeared nowhere in `Color.kt` — the icon
  was the one piece of identity never regenerated from the seeds.
- The **eye is a hole in the path**, not a shape painted in the background colour. It is wound
  against the other subpaths, so the non-zero fill rule both SVG and `VectorDrawable` use by
  default subtracts it. This matters for the **monochrome** layer, where Android tints everything
  one colour and a painted eye would simply disappear.
- Every subpath is stored **clockwise on screen** (positive shoelace area, y downward) and `hole`
  reverses it at read time, so the winding is a property of `nodes()` rather than of the stored
  data. `trace-mark.py` normalises to that convention on the way out.
- The mark is **near-square** (231×277 units), which is why `ART_SIZE` is 54dp and not the 60 the
  previous tall-narrow rabbit used: at 60 its rump and feet reach 36dp from the centre against the
  safe circle's 33dp radius, and a circular launcher would cut them. Shrinking to fit still leaves
  it carrying ~19% more ink than the mark it replaced, because a compact silhouette uses canvas a
  narrow one wastes.

## Why it is original art

The icon was previously Noto Emoji's rabbit (U+1F407) traced into `VectorDrawable`s. That is
permitted by the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, but on a condition that bit: the copyright notice
and licence text must reach whoever receives a copy, and a traced outline redistributed as art is
the font software, not merely output produced with it. Satisfying that needs an in-app licences
screen; the app has none, so the placeholder **blocked the Play upload**.

Drawing the mark ourselves removed the obligation and the placeholder in one move, which is why
`docs/PLAN.md` 3a preferred it over adding a screen for this alone. The Noto SVG and its licence
text are gone from the repo along with the traced paths.

The current mark keeps that property by a different route. `concept-1.png` was generated by an
image model from our own brief (`logo-brief.md`) — not a stock mark, an icon set, or an emoji
font — and `trace-mark.py` converted it to the curves in `rabbit.py`. Nothing arrives carrying
someone else's licence, which is the only property the Play upload ever depended on. Worth stating
plainly, though: "no third-party obligation" is a weaker claim than "we constructed it from
primitives", because it rests on the generator not having reproduced something recognisable rather
than on the shape having been derived from nothing. The mark is a generic sitting rabbit
silhouette, which is about as low-risk as that gets, and the source PNG is committed so the
provenance is inspectable rather than remembered.

One distinction worth keeping straight, because it looks like a contradiction: the feature
graphic's wordmark is *rendered* with Noto Sans, which is also OFL, and that is fine. The OFL
restricts redistributing **glyph outlines as art**; it explicitly does not restrict documents and
images produced by rendering text. Tracing the rabbit was the former. Setting a word in a typeface
is the latter.

## The feature graphic

1024×500, RGB with no alpha — Play rejects transparency. It reuses the icon's ground so the two
read as one identity, and carries a low-contrast weight-trend line with **irregular x spacing**,
the same honesty the chart itself is held to.

## Screenshots

Two, from the debug build on the Xiaomi with its sample-data fixture: All-bunnies Home showing the
trend flag, and the weight chart over 90 days. Placeholders, replaced at 3g once the app has
stopped changing, and Polish needs its own set — each locale's screenshots upload separately.

The 90-day range is deliberate. The fixture seeds one 250 g typo among 2.4 kg weighings, on
purpose, so the chart and trend flag can be reviewed against bad data — and over 1 year it
dominates the axis and squashes everything else flat. The chart is right to draw it; a store
listing is just the wrong place to show it.

The device is 1220×2712 (2.22:1), taller than the 9:16 Play documents for phone screenshots, so
`pad-screenshot.py` centres each on a 9:16 canvas filled with the screenshot's own edge colour. It
pads rather than crops because cropping a 2.22:1 shot to 9:16 would cut a fifth of the screen off.

## Regenerating

```bash
python3 art/trace-mark.py              # concept-1.png -> the curves pasted into rabbit.py
python3 art/make-launcher-icon.py      # VectorDrawables, notification icon, ten mipmaps, play-icon-512.png
python3 art/make-feature-graphic.py    # play-feature-graphic.png
python3 art/pad-screenshot.py in.png out.png
```

`trace-mark.py` is the only one whose output is **pasted** rather than written: it prints the two
path strings and `rabbit.py` holds them as `MARK` and `EYE`. That is deliberate — `rabbit.py` is
the single definition of the mark, and a definition a script overwrites on every run is one nobody
can hand-correct. It also means the tracer only has to be re-run when the concept art changes.

Unlike the icon's previous two scripts, these are **committed** — they are what gets re-run when
the identity changes or 3g retakes the screenshots, and a generator you have to reconstruct from a
README is a generator nobody re-runs.

The layout numbers:

- The mark's longest side is scaled to **54dp** on the 108dp canvas and centred on its bounding
  box. A launcher may mask down to the **66dp safe circle**, so what matters is how far the art
  itself reaches from the centre: **32.5dp against a 33dp radius**. `make-launcher-icon.py`
  measures that on every run and prints `inside` or `CLIPPED`. It used to reason about the
  bounding box's half-diagonal instead — a number that reports a clipping problem the empty
  corners never had, and that would have quietly missed this one when the mark changed shape.
- The **notification icon** scales the same path to fill 96 of the 108 viewport (a group scale of
  `NOTIFICATION_ART / ART_SIZE`), because a status-bar icon has no safe zone to respect and at
  54dp the rabbit would be a speck in a 24dp slot.
- The flat assets are cropped to the **central 72dp**, because that is all a launcher ever shows
  of an adaptive icon. Exporting the full 108dp square instead would make the listing icon look
  like the rabbit shrank.
- `ic_launcher_round.webp` gets a circular alpha mask; `play-icon-512.png` is flattened to RGB.
