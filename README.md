# rabbit-app

An Android app for keeping track of a rabbit's health.

Rabbits hide illness well, and the warning signs — a little less weight, smaller droppings, a skipped
meal — are easy to miss and easy to forget. This app is somewhere to write them down, for one rabbit or
several, so a change over weeks is visible instead of remembered.

**What it keeps track of**

- Weight over time, charted, with the change since last time shown in grams
- Observations: droppings, appetite, mood, activity, symptoms — logged when you notice something,
  not on a schedule
- Vet visits, and scans of the paperwork that comes back from them
- Medication courses, with reminders for doses and a record of what was actually given
- Reminders for recurring care — nail trims, vaccinations, weigh-ins
- Photos, because you will take them anyway

Rabbits that live together share a litter tray, so an observation can honestly cover more than one of them
rather than pretending the droppings belonged to whichever name you tapped.

**What it isn't**

Not medical advice. It records what you saw and shows you the trend; judging what that means is between
you and your vet.

**How it works**

Free, no ads, and no account. Everything is stored on the phone — there is no server, so nothing is
uploaded anywhere. Backups are exported to storage you choose, including your own cloud drive if you want
one. Android only.
